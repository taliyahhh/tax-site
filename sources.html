Where every number comes from

Every number on this site comes from a government agency's own published data. Federal, California, and New York numbers are typed in from official reports. Every other state, city, town, and county is pulled automatically from public datasets once a month.

What's left out

  • City-run utilities, airports, and seaports. Customer bills and fees pay for these, not taxes.
  • Federal money passed through to states. It's counted once, at the federal level.
  • School districts and special districts (like transit or water districts). They're separate governments with their own budgets.
  • Counties, unless the zip code has no city government. Where there's a city, the site shows the city, even though the county also serves those residents.

Keeping it current

City numbers are rebuilt automatically on the first of every month by a script that runs on GitHub (scripts/build-city-data.mjs). It downloads both datasets, sorts each line into a category, and saves the results with the site. Federal and state numbers are updated by hand when each new year's report comes out.

Numbers are rounded, and a few categories are judgment calls. This site is for learning, not tax or financial advice.
