    Fo

    fo /fō/ short for formula

    The rules the giant uses to split the pile. Some money follows formulas based on population and need. Some follows people, like Social Security. Some follows federal jobs and contracts.

    The giant's ledger2023

    Only three states paid in more than they got back.

    New Jersey, Massachusetts, and Washington. Every other state received more federal money than its people and businesses sent. New Jersey's gap was the biggest: $18.9 billion, about $2,011 a person.
